It's a [short story](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~gamvrosi/thelastq.html) by Isaac Asimov, and we decided to name our podcast after it. I draw a lot of optimism and hope from that story.

A few days before the Anthropic IPO and an [S-1 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm) a third of which warns about various ways AI could end the world, I feel that optimism. I feel a strong sense of hope and anxiety about the future.

Or maybe the future isn't AI, but rather an enhanced version of humanity, a great evolutionary leap free from the shackles of natural selection, as [Homo Deus](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31138556-homo-deus) puts it.

Whatever the future might hold, it is approaching at an accelerating pace. It is an exponential curve, and it is hard to grasp the true significance of its potential when you are looking at it only from the beginning.
